# Flowmingo

> Flowmingo is a free AI interviewer for recruiters. Each applicant interviews in their own time; the recruiter gets the recording, the transcript and a report scored against their own criteria. Unlimited AI interviews on every plan, in 60+ languages. CV evaluation, tests and a full applicant tracking system (ATS) come with it. Y Combinator-backed. Founded 2024, Singapore.
